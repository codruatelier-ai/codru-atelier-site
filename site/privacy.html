1. Businesses I write to

I write one-to-one to businesses about a problem visible on their own website, and only in countries where that is allowed without prior consent. I don't email businesses in countries that require consent first, sole traders or ordinary partnerships in the UK, or anyone who has said no.

  • What: the business name, its website, a business email and phone number published on that website or its public Google listing, and the facts I checked there.
  • Where from: the business's own website and its public Google listing. Never bought lists, never LinkedIn scraping.
  • Why, and the legal basis: to offer a relevant service to a business (legitimate interest, GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); UK PECR and US CAN-SPAM rules for business email).
  • How long: if you don't reply, everything is deleted after 90 days. At most two follow-ups, and nothing after a "no".
  • Stopping it: reply "no thanks" to any email. I keep only a one-way scrambled (hashed) copy of your address, forever, so I never write again.
  • No tracking: no open-tracking pixels, no tracked links.

2. People who ask for an audit, a call or a proposal

  • Legal basis: your consent, and the steps you asked for before a possible contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and (b)).
  • How long: while we're in touch, and at most 12 months after the last contact, unless you become a client.

3. Clients
