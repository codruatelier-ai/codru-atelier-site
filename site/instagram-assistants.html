Instagram Growth

An assistant that replies to your Instagram messages and comments and books appointments for you.

What it does

A customer sends a message. The assistant answers their questions about your services, opening hours and prices, offers free times from your calendar and books the slot. It can also move or cancel a booking. When someone comments a keyword on a post, it replies to them privately. If it is unsure, it hands the chat to you.

What is included

Setup of the assistant with your services, prices and tone. Connection to your Cal.com calendar. Testing with you before it goes live. A short guide for you and your team. Support and updates while you subscribe.

How setup works

You connect your Instagram account through ManyChat, a Meta Business Partner, and you pay ManyChat directly (about $39 a month on its Pro plan). I set up the rest: the assistant, the calendar link and the rules. Setup takes about a week once your accounts are ready.

What you need

An Instagram business or creator account. A ManyChat account on a plan that includes Instagram automation. A Cal.com account with your appointment types. A list of your services, prices and the questions customers ask most.

Studio Assistant

A team of assistants for a tattoo studio. One install per studio, in Romanian and English.

What it does

It answers Instagram messages about styles, sizes and prices and helps clients book a consultation or session. After a session it sends aftercare advice. It keeps a waitlist and offers freed slots. It suggests ideas for your posts, and each morning it sends you a briefing of the day.

What is included

Setup for your studio: artists, styles, price rules, deposit policy, aftercare text and tone, in the languages you choose. Testing with you before launch. A guide for you and your artists. Support and updates while you subscribe.

How setup works

You connect Instagram through ManyChat, a Meta Business Partner, and you pay ManyChat directly (about $39 a month on its Pro plan). I set up the rest. Your studio gets its own separate install, so your data is not mixed with other studios.

What you need

An Instagram business or creator account. A ManyChat account on a plan that includes Instagram automation. A Cal.com account for bookings. Your price list, aftercare instructions and booking rules, written down or told to me on a call.

Questions

Do customers know it is an AI?

Yes. The assistant tells people it is an AI assistant. You also need to say so in your own channels. That is part of the terms.

Can I take over a chat?

Yes. You can pause the assistant for any conversation and reply yourself. When it cannot help, it tells the customer a person will follow up and alerts you.

What happens to my customers' data?

Messages are processed to answer and book, and kept only as long as needed. You stay in charge of your customers' data and I work on your instructions, under a written data processing agreement. See the privacy page for how this site handles data.

What if I stop?

Cancel with 30 days' notice. The software stays mine, and your data is returned or deleted on request.

Does it work with my phone and my calendar?

It works with Instagram through ManyChat and with Cal.com for bookings. It does not read your phone or other calendars.

Is it a fit for me?

If you take bookings and get repeat questions by message, probably. We check on the call, and if it is not a fit I say so.
